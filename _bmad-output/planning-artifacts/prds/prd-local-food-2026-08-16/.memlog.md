- (decision) Brain dump start: capability is vendor self-application to join local-food, not admin-unilateral creation (supersedes/extends FR-3 from prd-local-food-2026-08-10). Applicant fills request, agrees to Terms of Use, agrees to fees. Admin presumably reviews/approves. Example persona: Bill Green, apple orchard, wants to advertise/sell products via local-food. Business driver: platform has real operating costs, needs revenue to sustain operations or shuts down - this is a monetization requirement, not decorative.
- (decision) Money flow direction: local-food never pays vendors out. Vendors only pay local-food for platform usage. Fee model itself not yet decided.
- (decision) Terms of Use: placeholder for now, real legal content deferred.
- (decision) Application review: every application is reviewed, approved or rejected by admin. A NEEDS_INFO / follow-up-questions state exists - admin can ask applicant follow-up questions before approving, not just binary approve/reject.
- (decision) Fee model: flat recurring subscription, vendor pays platform. Order/checkout money flow unchanged - stays 100% to platform Stripe account as today, vendor fee is a fully separate charge.
- (decision) Stakes: hobby/solo (consistent with prd-local-food-2026-08-10). Mode: Coaching path.
- (decision) Entry point: Journey-led. Protagonist: Bill Green, apple orchard owner.
- (decision) Journey - Entry: Bill arrives from a marketing page. Application form fields: business name, product types, address, 2-3 sentence business description, how he plans to use local-food. Before/during application, applicant must read a clear disclosure page: what local-food is, how it works, and dependent third-party services used (Clerk, and others - Stripe/Twilio/Cloudinary implied) - so there's no ambiguity for the vendor later. Distinct from Terms of Use (still placeholder) - this is a services-dependency disclosure.
- (decision) Application requires accurate contact info: email + SMS/phone, collected directly on the form (not necessarily from Clerk). Every applying vendor gets assigned a 'success coach' - a specific Local Food team member responsible for that vendor's issues, not just a generic admin queue. Open: is success coach a distinct role, or the existing flat Admin role plus a per-vendor assignment field? Answered-once resolved: follow-up questions and the review queue route to the assigned coach specifically.
- (decision) Success coach = existing flat Admin role + a Vendor-to-assigned-Admin relationship. No new role/permission tier.
- (decision) Clerk timing: Bill applies first (form only, no account needed). Clerk account creation happens AFTER approval. This means a real invite/claim step now exists post-approval (vendor invited to sign up, gets bound to their Vendor record) - effectively supersedes the prior PRD's AD-8 'manual out-of-band binding, no invite flow' Deferred decision. Flag forward to architecture update.
- (decision) Full UJ-1 (Bill Green onboarding) captured: entry marketing page -> reads services-disclosure page -> submits application (business name, product types, address, 2-3 sentence description, planned use, accurate email+SMS contact) -> redirected to a status page, updates via email+SMS -> lands in review queue assigned to a success coach (Admin) -> coach may ask follow-up questions before deciding -> approved/rejected -> on approval: Clerk signup invite -> subscription billing setup -> storefront setup -> CLIMAX: storefront goes live at /vendors/{slug}. First-sale is a separate, later journey, not required for this PRD.
- (decision) Billing UI: Stripe hosted Customer Portal, no custom billing management page. Rejection: status page + email/SMS, reason optional. Billing lapse: storefront deactivates via existing AD-4 soft-delete pattern (reused from prd-local-food-2026-08-10), not new mechanism. Discovery complete, moving to draft.
